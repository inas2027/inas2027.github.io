### 19th Annual Conference of the
# International Network of Analytical Sociology
### May 19-21 2027 | Toulouse

[Institute for Advanced Study in Toulouse](https://www.iast.fr/)

[Toulouse School of Economics](https://www.tse-fr.eu/)

> The annual conference of the International Network of Analytical Sociology (INAS) brings 
> together scholars from quantitative sociology and adjacent fields (e.g., demography, 
> political science, computational social science) who focus on mechanism-based explanations 
> for social phenomena. Analytical sociologists study a range of topics, from segregation, 
> inequality and polarization, to migration, social norms and the diffusion of innovation. 
> 
> The 19th edition of INAS takes place at the Institute for Advanced Study in Toulouse---*la ville rose*, 
> France's fourth city by population. Toulouse is a vibrant city with a beautiful historic center, and is located in 
> the heart of the Occitanie region, at the foot of the Pyrenees and in between the Atlantic 
> and Mediterranean coasts.

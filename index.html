About the Conference

The annual conference of the International Network of Analytical Sociology (INAS) brings together scholars from quantitative sociology and adjacent fields (e.g., demography, political science, computational social science) who focus on mechanism-based explanations for social phenomena. Analytical sociologists study a range of topics, from segregation, inequality and polarization, to migration, social norms and the diffusion of innovation.


The 19th edition of INAS takes place at the Institute for Advanced Study in Toulouse—la ville rose, France's fourth city by population. Toulouse is a vibrant city with a beautiful historic center, and is located in the heart of the Occitanie region, at the foot of the Pyrenees and in between the Atlantic and Mediterranean coasts.

Women's forum

The Women’s Forum aims to enhance recognition of the work, perspectives, and expertise of women within the broader analytical sociology community. We will meet on May 19th in the afternoon and host flash talks for junior scholars as well as a panel on women in academia. The event is open to everyone. It is an informal setting for people to get to know each other and we will go for drinks afterwards.
